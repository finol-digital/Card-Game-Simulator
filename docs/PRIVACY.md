# Card Game Simulator Privacy Policy

Card Game Simulator is developed and published by Finol Digital LLC. We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. You can visit this website and use the application without creating an account. The website does not use advertising or behavioral-tracking cookies. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to provide so that we can respond to your request. The application may send anonymous crash or exception information when a failure occurs; this information is used to diagnose and improve reliability.

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share information only with service providers that help operate the service, when required by law, or when necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Finol Digital, our users, or others. Links to app stores, community sites, and other third-party services are governed by those services' own privacy policies. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information through support channels.

If you have a privacy question, want to request access to or deletion of information you provided to us, or need to report a concern, contact [david@finoldigital.com](mailto:david@finoldigital.com). We may update this policy when our practices change; the current version is published on this page.
